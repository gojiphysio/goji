Bookings vs recognised revenue Live

Bookings = cash invoiced when a patient pays (including packages upfront). Recognised = revenue actually earned as sessions are delivered. The gap between the bars is your deferred revenue — money in the bank that you still owe in sessions. A wide gap is fine if it's closing week-on-week. Watch for a gap that stops shrinking.

Per-clinician performance Live

Bookings ($ invoiced) vs recognised revenue ($ earned through delivered sessions), last 30 days. The gap is deferred revenue: packages committed but sessions not yet used. A large gap can mean strong forward sales — or a churn risk if patients stop coming in. Clinician utilisation % (slots filled ÷ slots available) requires appointment data — flagged for Charles.
