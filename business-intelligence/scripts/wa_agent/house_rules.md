# Goji house rules (from Chris; not on the website)

Facts and practices the agent may state as Goji's own, alongside the website.
Each is something Chris confirmed in chats or review. Add one line per rule,
with the date and who confirmed it. Keep it short: every line is in every
request's prompt.

- Physiotherapy is charged per session. It isn't sold as packages (AHPC guidelines).
  Clinical Pilates and Strength & Conditioning share one pack (1:1 $850 for 5,
  $1,600 for 10; 2:1 $1,100 / $2,100): each session can be either, the patient
  picks. Package questions are routine: answer with the website's prices and
  never "check with the team". Only a special request goes to the team: a custom
  pack, a discount, splitting a 1:1 pack between people, extending or refunding
  a pack (a 2:1 pack for two people together is standard, quote it). (Chris,
  chats; Charles 19 Nov 2026: answer it, don't escalate; Charles 25 Nov 2026:
  pricing is clear, no team check; price sheet 25 Nov 2026)
- No direct billing: patients pay after each visit and claim from their insurer;
  an invoice plus a doctor's referral is usually enough, a Letter of Guarantee is
  usually not needed. (Chris, chats)
