Bookings vs recognised revenue Live

Running totals since the start of the selected period. Bookings = cumulative cash invoiced (packages counted upfront). Recognised = cumulative revenue earned as sessions are delivered. The shaded gap between the curves = outstanding deferred revenue at that point in time. A widening gap means you're selling ahead of delivery. Watch for a gap that stops growing — it means patients aren't coming back to burn their packs.

Per-clinician performance Live
