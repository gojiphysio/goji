## KPKB: revision pending

The fictional name KPKB and red OCBC-inspired visual family are selected.
The hexagonal badge/bar-chart version is rejected, not an approved asset.
For a requested revision, restore more of the circular nautical character while
changing the main mark's geometry. Preserve red lettering and colours. Do not
revert to the exact original ship emblem. Save candidates separately for review.

Other established vehicle brands (SOS Transit, CONFRONT and GRIP) are governed by
the Goji GTA video skill and their separate vehicle reference manifests. This
library does not replace those approved references.
