## 1. Compliance landscape (the constraint that shapes everything)

Full detail in `03-compliance-guardrails.md`. Headlines:

- **Physiotherapy's licensable status under the phased HCSA is genuinely ambiguous** - one MOH FAQ lists physio as non-licensable; the HCSA expanded coverage to allied health and is rolled out in phases. **Must verify Goji's status with MOH.**
- **AHPC Code of Professional Conduct** always binds the physios: advertising must be truthful, accurate, and not induce unnecessary demand; no claims of being "better than others" (kills superlatives/comparatives); no package deals/discounts that induce unnecessary demand.
- **Medicines (Advertisement and Sale) Act** restricts advertising treatment of a condition.
- **Strict HCSA list** (if licensable): no laudatory claims, no before/after in ads, no outcome/time promises, no discounts/packages/free in ads, no paid reviews/endorsements, testimonials only if given directly to the clinic and unedited; no reposting patients' own posts.
- **Fitness/wellness (Pilates, S&C for general wellbeing) sits outside healthcare advertising** and gets normal consumer-marketing freedom - the basis for our two-lane model.

Sources: [MOH HCS (Advertisement) Regulations FAQ](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/7/a2d9019f-7617-40f1-8788-7e105b0a77e9/12-10-2023-advert-regs-faqs-(1).pdf), [MOH ask.gov.sg - non-licensable services](https://ask.gov.sg/moh/questions/clu6lx3rt00bq314r3yj38ixy), [AHPC Code of Professional Conduct](https://www.healthprofessionals.gov.sg/ahpc/information-for-registered-allied-health-professionals/code-of-professional-conduct), [First Page Digital - SG medical marketing guidelines](https://www.firstpagedigital.sg/resources/business/singapores-medical-marketing-regulations-guidelines/).

## 3. The referral / partnership ecosystem

Natural upstream referrers for post-partum physio, ranked by proximity to the decision [ranking inferred, providers cited]:

1. **OBGYNs / gynae clinics & KKH** - clinical referral gold standard; KKH runs women's-health physio. 
2. **Confinement nanny agencies & centres** - PEM, NannySOS, Super Nanny, NewLife; very high reach to mums in weeks 1-4 post-partum. [PEM](https://pemconfinement.com/), [NannySOS](https://www.nannysos.com.sg/)
3. **Postnatal hubs** - Mother & Child (IBCLC lactation, doulas, yoga/pilates under one roof) - one partner, concentrated audience. [Mother & Child](https://www.motherandchild.com.sg/)
4. **Doulas & lactation consultants** - Beloved Bumps, Wonder Births, Papaya Wellness. [Sassy Mama doulas](https://www.sassymamasg.com/pregnancy/ultimate-guide-to-doulas-in-singapore/)
5. **Prenatal yoga/pilates studios, mum-and-baby cafes, paediatric clinics** [standard adjacency].

How SG clinics build these: cross-referral agreements, co-hosted workshops, listing on partner resource pages, reciprocal social tagging.

## 5. Content that resonates (women's health, SG)

- **"Is this normal?" / myth-busting** - leaking, ab separation, painful sex, "snapping back." Reassurance-led education converts in forums and Reels.
- **Pelvic-floor + diastasis explainers** and **safe return-to-exercise** progressions.
- **Practitioner-led, authentic short video** beats glossy ads (mirrors the SG momfluencer authenticity trend).
- **Cultural nuance:** confinement is near-universal across ethnicities (~85-96% of mums observe it ~3 weeks post-partum); younger mums increasingly modify it. Position physio as **complementary to confinement, not replacing it** ("what to do after confinement"). Lead in English; consider Mandarin/Malay/Tamil touches for confinement-adjacent content. [Confinement practices study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4992357/)

## 6. What works on social for wellness/fitness brands

- **Benchmarks:** Alo Yoga / Lululemon sell *lifestyle + community*, not products (serene IG aesthetic + bite-sized tutorials on TikTok). A clinical/physio background "adds credibility and premium appeal" - credentials are an asset. [Creator Hero](https://www.creator-hero.com/blog/top-instagram-fitness-brands)
- **SG boutique scene:** Ritual Gym, GymmBoxx, KX Pilates compete on experience and accessibility - most ignore women's/family specificity, which is Goji's gap to own. [ZULA boutique studios](https://zula.sg/boutique-fitness-studios/)
- **Formats (2025-26):** Reels = reach/discovery; carousels = saves + authority (highest save rate, ideal for education). Use Reels to discover, carousels to nurture. [CreatorsJet](https://www.creatorsjet.com/blog/instagram-reels-vs-carousels-vs-images)
- **Hooks/length:** up to 50% drop in first 3 seconds; retention beats length (10s at 80% beats 60s at 30%). ~85% watch sound-off, so on-screen text is mandatory; captions are now searchable (SEO); use 2-3 relevant hashtags. [TrueFuture](https://www.truefuturemedia.com/articles/instagram-reels-reach-2026-business-growth-guide)
- **What drives bookings vs vanity:** "Educate first, show their face second, ask for the booking third." Practitioner explainers (60-90s), "what to expect" room walkthroughs, real (consented) stories convert; motivational quotes and stock photos do not. [LXP Digital](https://www.lxp-digital.com/post/social-media-marketing-for-clinics-what-actually-brings-patients)
- **Suggested content mix [synthesis]:** ~40% practitioner education/myth-busting, 25% behind-the-scenes/warmth, 20% patient stories & "what to expect", 15% movement/exercise demos.
- **Cadence (realistic for a small clinic):** start 2-3 Reels + 1-2 carousels/week, one monthly batch-film day. Best windows Wed/Thu, mornings and evenings SGT.

## 7. Local discovery (GBP + SEO)

- **GBP is the highest-leverage local asset.** Precise primary category (Physical therapy clinic) + secondaries (women's health, sports medicine, fitness centre); booking link; complete hours; real interior/team photos; active Posts (2-3x/week) and Q&A. Profiles with photos get ~42% more direction requests and ~35% more website clicks. [Lead to Conversion](https://leadtoconversion.com/blog/google-business-profile-for-healthcare-services/)
- **Reviews drive ranking + conversion.** Steady velocity + owner responses matter. Compliance tension: ask satisfied patients to share honest feedback but never incentivise. [practicebeat](https://www.practicebeat.com/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-healthcare-marketing)
- **"Physio near me"** ranking = GBP completeness + proximity + NAP consistency (keep Name/Address/Phone identical everywhere). Build condition/neighbourhood landing pages. Expect 2-4 months to first movement, 4-6 to stable top-3. [causalfunnel](https://www.causalfunnel.com/blog/top-10-local-seo-strategies-for-physiotherapists-in-2026/)

- **PhysioActive, Health2Mama, Physio Down Under, Hello Physio, UrbanRehab** - women's-health physio bolted onto general physio. [Homage round-up](https://www.homage.sg/resources/physiotherapy-clinics-women-singapore/)
- **Calyx** - on-the-ground competitor known to the team.
- **Physio & Sole** - on-the-ground competitor known to the team.
- **Core Concepts** - largest private physio group (generalist scale player).

**Read:** dedicated women's-only positioning is largely held by Embrace; most others treat women's health as an add-on. **Goji's post-partum + family + movement (Pilates/S&C) combination is a differentiable wedge** — and Goji can out-educate and out-show-up the bolt-on players. Katong is an intentional SEO/GEO neighbourhood target: ranking there puts Goji in front of Embrace's prospective patients.

## Paid advertising platform restrictions

- **Meta (Instagram/Facebook):** "pelvic floor" and "pelvic health" are flagged as non-advertisable by Meta Business Suite. Any paid campaign targeting pelvic conditions will be blocked. Pelvic/women's-health content must stay organic on Meta. Paid Meta budget should target MSK terms, neighbourhood + parenting interests, and Lane B fitness offers only.
- **Google Search:** No equivalent block on pelvic health or physio terms. High-intent search terms (e.g. "postnatal physio Singapore", "pelvic floor physiotherapist", "back pain physio Lentor") are all runnable with compliant ad copy.

## Channel-by-channel email / WhatsApp / GBP best practice

- **WhatsApp:** ~98% open vs ~20% email. Consent first; integrate with email/SMS; do not over-message. In SG, treat WhatsApp as the primary booking/reminder/nurture channel. [respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-for-healthcare)
- **Email:** segment by condition/treatment/appointment type; personalise; automate reminders + education. [Optimantra](https://www.optimantra.com/blog/healthcare-email-marketing-examples-and-best-practices)
- **GBP posts:** repurpose Reels/carousels at zero extra cost; 2-3x/week lifts visibility.
