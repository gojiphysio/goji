## The 5 campaigns

### Campaign 1 — "Is This Normal?" (Women's Health Wedge)

**What:** A pelvic floor and post-partum education content series. Answers the questions women type late at night — "is leaking after birth normal?", "diastasis recti explained", "pelvic-girdle pain in pregnancy" — before they ever book.

**Channel:** Organic Instagram (carousels + Reels) and GBP posts. **NOT Meta paid** — "pelvic floor" and "pelvic health" are flagged as non-advertisable by Meta Business Suite. All pelvic content stays organic.

**Content workflow:** Claude drafts → Cheryl reviews for clinical accuracy → publish. Reduces creation burden; maintains clinical authority.

**Format priority:** Start with carousels (higher save rate, lower production threshold). Add Reels once the rhythm holds. Aim for 3-4 pieces this month.

**Anchor asset:** Pelvic floor condition landing page on the website (GEO-ready, FAQ block, Cheryl's byline).

**Why first:** This is the brand's sharpest point. It's underserved by general clinics, it's the content AI engines cite, and it feeds the partner outreach in Week 4.

---

### Campaign 2 — Clinic Discovery Foundation (GBP + Website + GEO)

**What:** Get every surface a new patient could find us on into working order — fully optimised, consistent, and structured for AI citation.

**GBP tasks (Week 2):**
- Primary category: Physical Therapy Clinic. Secondary: women's health, fitness centre.
- Complete hours, booking link, services list, Q&A seeded.
- 10+ real interior and team photos uploaded.
- 2-3 posts/week from here on (repurpose content from Campaign 1 + 3).

**Website tasks (Week 2):**
- Canonical brand name locked everywhere: "Goji Physio" (short, IG + website), "Goji Women's and Family Physiotherapy" (Google, formal). "Goji Health Pte Ltd" stays hidden.
- NAP consistent: same name, address, phone on website, GBP, Instagram, LinkedIn, health directories.
- 3-5 condition landing pages live: **post-partum recovery**, **pelvic floor**, **back pain**, **neck & shoulder**. Each page GEO-ready: Cheryl's byline + credentials at top, short answer block under headline, FAQ section (4-6 natural-language questions), question-phrased subheadings, date published, structured data (JSON-LD).
- Structured data types: MedicalClinic (homepage), MedicalWebPage + Article + Person (condition pages), FAQPage (any page with FAQs), Service (each treatment page).

**SEO neighbourhood targets:** Lentor (home base) AND Katong (intentional — Embrace's territory; showing up there puts us in front of their prospective patients).

**Output:** Done by end of Week 2.

### Campaign 3 — MSK Volume Engine

**What:** Back pain, neck/shoulder, desk injury, and sports injury content. The broadest reach channel and the highest-volume booking driver.

**Channel:** Organic Instagram + Google Search ads. No Meta restrictions on MSK terms, so this is the one pillar where paid can amplify organic from Week 6 onward.

**Paid (Month 1 → Week 6+):** ~70% of the SGD 800-1,200 Month 1 paid budget goes to Google Search on high-intent terms: "back pain physio Singapore", "physio near me Lentor", "neck and shoulder physio", branded variants. Compliant ad copy — no "best", no guarantees.

**Content:** 1-2 pieces/week from Week 3 alongside Campaign 1 content.

**Why in parallel with the wedge:** MSK fills the schedule. It pays the bills while the wedge builds authority.

---

### Campaign 4 — Partner Seeding

**What:** First outreach to 4-5 post-partum ecosystem partners. A warm, short intro: who Goji is, who we help, what a partnership looks like (cross-referral, co-hosted workshops, reciprocal social).

**Targets (Lentor + Katong catchment):**
1. OBGYNs / gynae clinics nearby
2. Confinement agencies (PEM, NannySOS, Super Nanny)
3. Doulas / lactation consultants (Beloved Bumps, Wonder Births, Papaya Wellness)
4. Postnatal hubs (Mother & Child)
5. Prenatal yoga or Pilates studios

**Timing:** Week 4. Needs some content live first — the intro lands better with three pieces of published content to point to.

**Output:** 4-5 intro messages sent; 2-3 conversations open by end of Month 1. Convert 2-3 into concrete agreements in Month 2.

---

### Campaign 5 — Discovery Rank Baseline (GEO North Star)

**What:** Stand up the Discovery Rank Sweep routine on the dashboard. Run the first sweep. Every week from here forward measures against this baseline.

**The routine:**
1. Load the priority prompt set — 15-20 patient questions we want to own. Include Lentor AND Katong neighbourhood variants. Examples:
   - "Best postnatal physio in Singapore"
   - "Pelvic floor physio near me Singapore"
   - "Is leaking after birth normal?"
   - "Diastasis recti recovery Singapore"
   - "Women's health physio Lentor"
   - "Women's health physio Katong"
   - "Postnatal Pilates Singapore"
   - "Returning to exercise after birth Singapore"
   - "Physio for pelvic-girdle pain pregnancy"
   - "Back pain physio Lentor / Singapore"
2. Hit each engine with the prompt: Google, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, TikTok, Instagram.
3. Log whether Goji appears in top 5 and at what position.
4. Write a snapshot row per (date, engine, prompt) into the dashboard discovery table.
5. Roll up to a Discovery Rank Score per engine (% of prompts where Goji appears top 5) and an overall score.

**Note:** This is a dashboard build task, not just a strategy task. The metric is not yet on the dashboard — needs to be instrumented in parallel with the website work.

**Output:** First sweep run, baseline row in dashboard, by end of Week 4.

## Fitness positioning — what Pilates and S&C are NOT in Month 1

Pilates and S&C are **not a campaign** and not a Month 1 reach driver. They are:

- A **relationship-extension tool** — how a physio patient keeps working with us after their course ends
- A **Lane B asset** — for future intro offers and founding-member campaigns (Month 3 onward)
- A **pull signal** for millennial women — acknowledge it as part of the offering, but don't lead with it

Do not lead any Month 1 copy with fitness. Every top-of-funnel piece leads with physio, women's health, or MSK.

## Open item before any Lane A campaign goes live

Goji is **AHPC-licensed** (each physio individually) but **not MOH-business-licensed**. This means the HCSA strict advertising list is best practice, not law. The binding floor is the AHPC Code of Professional Conduct. This slightly widens the lane — but confirm acceptable wording for borderline Lane A claims with AHPC or a healthcare lawyer before the first pelvic-floor or post-partum campaign piece goes out.
