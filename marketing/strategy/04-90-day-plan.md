# 90-Day Campaign Plan

Month-by-month arc with weekly sprints. Built on the strategy in `01-campaign-strategy.md`. Every published asset passes the lane-aware checklist in `03-compliance-guardrails.md`.

Theme of the quarter: **Get discoverable, get credible, get booked.**

- **Month 1 - FOUNDATION & AUTHORITY:** fix the discoverable surfaces, stand up the content engine, plant the wedge.
- **Month 2 - REACH & COMMUNITY:** show up where mums are, ramp content, light the partnership and workshop fires, begin modest paid.
- **Month 3 - CONVERSION & SCALE:** turn attention into bookings, formalize referral loops, scale winners, measure and plan Q2.

A note on sequencing: discoverability (GBP, SEO, website) compounds slowly (2-4 months to first movement), so we start it on day 1. Content and partnerships produce faster. Paid is layered only once organic shows what resonates.

---

## Month 1 - Foundation & Authority

**Goal:** every place a prospect could find Goji is optimized, the content engine runs, and the women's-health wedge is visibly planted.

### Week 1 - Audit & instrument
- Pull baselines from the BI dashboard (patients, visits, bookings) and from IG/GBP/site analytics into the operator KPI sheet.
- Confirm the pivotal compliance item with MOH: is Goji's physio licensable under HCSA? (Decides Lane A latitude.)
- GBP audit: primary category (Physical therapy clinic) + secondaries; complete hours; booking link; 10+ real interior/team photos; services list; Q&A seeded.
- Set up enquiry-source tagging (WhatsApp, calls, form) so we can attribute first visits.
- Decide the month's content themes (wedge + MSK led).

### Week 2 - Build the surfaces
- Website: create/upgrade condition landing pages - start with **post-partum recovery**, **pelvic floor**, and the top 2 MSK conditions (e.g. back pain, neck/shoulder). NAP consistent everywhere.
- Draft the Instagram bio, highlights (Conditions, What to expect, Meet the team, FAQ), and grid plan.
- Stand up the WhatsApp nurture basics (greeting, FAQ quick-replies, booking flow) with consent capture.
- Build the first batch of templates in the Claude Design app from the design-system brief.

### Week 3 - First batch film + publish
- One **batch film day**: capture 6-8 practitioner Reels (wedge + MSK education, "is this normal?", "what to expect at your first visit").
- Begin publishing: 2-3 Reels + 1-2 carousels/week. GBP posts 2-3x/week (repurpose).
- Start asking satisfied patients for honest Google reviews (never incentivised).
- Identify and list Goji for editorial round-ups (Sassy Mama, HoneyKids, Little Steps).

### Week 4 - Partnership outreach begins
- Shortlist 8-10 referral partners (gynae, confinement, doulas, lactation, prenatal studios) - see `partnerships/partnerships-and-events.md`. Send first 4-5 intros.
- Email/WhatsApp the existing list: a warm "what we do / who we help" educational note (not a promo on the clinical side).
- Review week-1 leading indicators; note early content winners.

**Month 1 exit criteria:** GBP fully optimized; 3-5 condition pages live; content engine producing weekly; first reviews coming in; 4-5 partnership conversations open; baselines instrumented.

## Month 2 - Reach & Community

**Goal:** expand reach where mums actually are, convert authority into enquiries, and start modest paid.

### Week 5 - Ramp content + first workshop planned
- Increase to 3 Reels + 2 carousels/week if sustainable. Double down on month-1 winners.
- Plan a **workshop**: in-clinic or partner-hosted, e.g. "Returning to exercise after birth - what's safe and when" (education-led, free or low-cost, Lane-A-careful framing). Set a date in week 7-8.
- Convert 2-3 partnership conversations into concrete cross-referral or co-hosting agreements.

### Week 6 - Launch modest paid
- Google Search live: high-intent terms (women's physio, postnatal physio, pelvic floor physiotherapy, physiotherapy [neighbourhood], branded). Compliant ad copy.
- Meta retargeting live: site + IG engagers. Lane A creative = education; Lane B = a clean fitness intro offer (founding-member / intro Pilates).
- Seed value into 1-2 mum communities via partners (not direct ads): a genuinely useful post or a partner sharing a Goji explainer.

### Week 7 - Workshop + community
- Run the workshop. Capture (consented) content from it for reuse. Collect enquiries.
- Begin a genuine, unpaid practitioner collaboration with a micro mum-creator (educational appearance, not a paid testimonial).
- GBP: post the workshop, add fresh photos, keep review velocity steady.

### Week 8 - Optimize the funnel
- Review: which content drives saves, profile visits, DMs? Which paid terms drive enquiries cheapest?
- Tighten the enquiry-to-first-visit handoff (WhatsApp response time, booking friction).
- Second batch film day, informed by what's working.

**Month 2 exit criteria:** reach up 2-3x; first workshop done with content captured; 2-3 live partnerships; paid running with an emerging cost-per-enquiry; enquiries trending up.

---

## Month 3 - Conversion & Scale

**Goal:** maximize first visits and active patients, formalize the referral engine, and set up Q2.

### Week 9 - Scale winners
- Reallocate paid toward the cheapest source of first visits. Increase budget on proven terms/creatives.
- Promote the fitness adjunct properly (Lane B freedom): founding-member / intro-class campaign as the "return to movement" bridge and a retention driver.

### Week 10 - Referral engine + seasonal tie-in
- Formalize the referral loop: simple referral cards/links for partners, a shared workshop calendar, reciprocal social.
- If a baby fair or seasonal window lands in-quarter, plan presence (booth or co-hosted talk). Otherwise run a seasonal content campaign (e.g. a themed education series).

### Week 11 - Stories & reactivation
- Publish 1-2 (fully consented, compliance-checked) patient stories framed as education, not cure.
- Reactivation push to the existing list: a "haven't seen you in a while / here's what's new" nurture (Lane B offers allowed; Lane A stays educational).
- Second workshop or a partner co-hosted session.

### Week 12 - Measure & plan Q2
- Full readout against north-star KPIs from the BI dashboard: new patients, active patients, visits, bookings, treatment-mix shift.
- Cost-per-first-visit by channel. What to keep, cut, scale.
- Write the Q2 plan: which pillar to push, whether to grow the fitness mix deliberately, partnership expansion, paid scale-up.

**Month 3 exit criteria:** measurable lift in enquiries and first visits; known cost-per-first-visit by channel; 3-5 live partnerships and a repeatable referral loop; a data-backed Q2 plan.

## The weekly operating rhythm (every week, all quarter)

- **Mon:** review last week's leading indicators; set the week's posts from the calendar.
- **Tue-Thu:** publish Reels/carousels (best windows Wed/Thu AM & PM SGT); 2-3 GBP posts across the week.
- **Daily:** reply to every DM, comment, review, and enquiry fast (WhatsApp is the booking workhorse).
- **Fri:** advance one partnership; check paid spend/pacing; capture any clinic moments for content.
- **Monthly:** one batch film day; BI north-star review; plan next month's themes.

## Risks & mitigations

- **Compliance misstep** -> every asset runs the lane-aware checklist; verify the HCSA status early.
- **Content cadence slips** (small team) -> batch filming + a stocked copy bank + repurposing across IG/GBP/email reduce per-week load.
- **Marketing ahead of delivery** -> keep fitness as adjunct, not hero, until the mix actually shifts; don't pump demand we can't serve.
- **Attribution gaps** -> tag enquiry sources from day 1; the BI dashboard closes the loop on first visits.
