# Partnerships & Events - The Post-Partum Referral Engine

The women's-health ecosystem in Singapore is concentrated: a handful of partner types and events reach most of the audience. Borrowing their trusted audiences is the highest-leverage off-platform play for the wedge. Sources and detail in `../strategy/02-market-research.md`.

Compliance note: partnerships are mostly Lane A (clinical). Genuine cross-referrals and co-hosted education are fine. **No paid endorsements, no incentivised referrals framed as inducements, no testimonials that breach the rules.** When a partner shares Goji content, keep it educational.

## Partner types, ranked by proximity to the decision

| Rank | Partner type | Why | Examples to approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | OBGYNs / gynae clinics, KKH | Clinical referral gold standard; closest to the decision | Local gynae practices near the clinic; KKH women's-health physio network |
| 2 | Confinement nanny agencies & centres | Reach mums in weeks 1-4 post-partum | PEM, NannySOS, Super Nanny, NewLife |
| 3 | Postnatal hubs (multi-service) | One partner, concentrated audience | Mother & Child (lactation, doulas, yoga/pilates) |
| 4 | Doulas & lactation consultants | Trusted, early, personal | Beloved Bumps, Wonder Births, Papaya Wellness |
| 5 | Prenatal yoga/pilates studios | Adjacent movement audience | Local prenatal studios |
| 6 | Paediatric clinics, mum-and-baby cafes | Family breadth, ongoing touchpoints | Neighbourhood paeds; family cafes |

## How to build each relationship

1. **Warm intro** - a short, human note from a practitioner (not a sales pitch): who Goji is, who we help, and an offer to be a referral resource for their clients' women's-health/recovery needs.
2. **Make it easy** - a one-pager on what Goji treats and how to refer; a simple referral link/card.
3. **Reciprocate** - refer back where appropriate; tag and share their content; feature them in a workshop.
4. **Co-create** - co-host an education workshop (e.g. a confinement centre hosts "recovery after confinement"; a prenatal studio hosts "preparing your body for birth").
5. **Stay in touch** - a light quarterly check-in; share useful content they can pass on.

## Editorial round-ups (credibility, not paid)

Pursue listings in: Sassy Mama SG, HoneyKids Asia, Little Steps Asia ("best postnatal physio / pelvic-floor expert" round-ups). These are editorial - we can be included and pitch our expertise, but we cannot pay for a "best" endorsement (compliance + credibility). Pitch angle: a practitioner available for expert commentary on women's-health topics. Tracker and drafts: `roundup-outreach.md`.

## Workshops (trust at scale)

- **Format:** education-led, free or low-cost, Lane-A-careful framing. Capture (consented) content for reuse.
- **Topics:** "Returning to exercise after birth - what's safe and when," "Preparing your body for birth," "Pelvic floor 101," "Staying strong as you age."
- **Where:** in-clinic, or co-hosted at a partner (confinement centre, prenatal studio, family cafe).
- **Cadence:** ~one per month from month 2. Each workshop = promo posts + registration page + on-the-day content + follow-up email.

## Events & baby fairs (built-in pregnant-mum capture)

Singapore baby fairs cluster Q1 and mid-year. Evaluate presence (booth or co-hosted talk) against budget and timing within the 90 days.

| Event | Venue | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mummys Market | Singapore Expo | Largest SEA baby fair; recurring (e.g. Apr 2026); goodie bags for first ~1,500 expectant mums |
| Babyland Mega Baby Fair | Singapore Expo | Recurring one-stop baby fair |
| Pregnant and Popped | varies | Expert-led pregnancy/postpartum workshops - a natural slot for a Goji talk |

Verify current dates before planning. A co-hosted expert talk is often higher-leverage (and more compliant) than a hard-sell booth. Align any event push with the relevant content theme that week.

## Community seeding (do it right)

- Mum communities live on Facebook groups, Telegram/WhatsApp EDD groups, and forums (theAsianparent, KiasuParents, SingaporeMotherhood).
- **Do not** spam invite-only groups with ads. **Do** let partners or genuine members share a Goji explainer when it's genuinely useful, and be present where editorial/Q&A is welcome.
- A practitioner answering questions helpfully (where allowed) builds more trust than any ad.
