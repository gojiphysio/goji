# Roundup outreach - tracker and pitches

Editorial "top clinics" roundups are what Google's AI Overviews and Claude
build their women's-health clinic lists from (audits 2026-10-12 and
2026-10-26 in `../seo/audits/`). Goji is in none of them yet. Why each target
matters: `../seo/seo-geo-review.md` §4.5.

Rules for every pitch:

- Editorial only. We never pay for inclusion or for a "best" label, and we
  never ask to be called best (`../strategy/03-compliance-guardrails.md`).
- Send from hello@gojiphysio.com, with the NAP exactly as in
  `../seo/gbp-listing.md`.
- Link the website as `https://www.gojiphysio.com/` (an editorial citation,
  not a CTA). If an outlet wants a WhatsApp or booking link, give a
  `go.gojiphysio.com` slug, never raw `wa.me` or Plato.
- Clinic facts (prices, team, services) come from `public/llms.txt` on the
  site. Re-check prices against `SITE.pricesAsOf` before sending.
