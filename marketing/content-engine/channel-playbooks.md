# Channel Playbooks

How to run each channel week-to-week. The operator owns delivery; these are the standing plays. All assets pass the lane-aware checklist in `../strategy/03-compliance-guardrails.md`.

---

## Instagram (primary reach + authority)

**Role:** discovery via Reels, authority via carousels, warmth via stories.

- **Profile:** clear bio (who we help + location + booking link), highlights (Conditions, What to expect, Meet the team, FAQ, Workshops), brand-consistent grid. See **Profile / bio for reach** below.

### Profile / bio for reach

The bio is the most-indexed text on the profile and where we plant the women's-health wedge. Lead with the wedge terms (our priority SEO set and our differentiator vs the bolt-on clinics), then signal the wider scope so MSK and family searchers still see themselves.

- **Name field is the lever.** IG search weights the bold display-name field (~30 chars) and handle far above the body text. Spend the Name field on the highest-intent wedge terms, e.g. `Goji · Women's & Postnatal Physio SG`. MSK / "physio near me" reach is carried by the handle, captions, GBP, and condition landing pages — no need to cram them into the bio.
- **Body keywords:** include the real services as factual descriptors — *pre-natal, post-natal, pelvic floor* (women's wedge), plus *back & neck* (MSK) and *staying strong* (family / active-aging), the Lentor neighbourhood term, and the brand promise. Write the terms a mum would search.
- **CTA:** route the link through the dashboard `GET /r/<slug>` redirect → WhatsApp (tracked), never a raw `wa.me` link.
- **Compliance (bio is advertising → Lane A):** these terms are a *factual description of services we genuinely deliver* — truthful and accurate (AHPC), an explicit "Clear YES" in `../strategy/03-compliance-guardrails.md`. Keep clear of the Lane A Hard-NOs: no *fix/cure/treat your [condition]*, no *best / leading / #1 / the only*.
- **Reference bio:**
  > **Name:** Goji · Women's & Postnatal Physio 🇸🇬
  > Women's, postnatal & family physiotherapy + Pilates · Lentor, SG
  > Pre-natal · pelvic floor · back & neck · staying strong
  > Helping you feel confident in your body again 💛
  > 👇 Message us on WhatsApp
- **Reels** for reach: 3-line script (hook/middle/takeaway), 15-90s, retention over length, on-screen text always, hook in first 3 seconds.
- **Carousels** for saves/authority: one idea in 5-8 slides, berry-dot bullets, soft CTA on the last slide.
- **Captions** are searchable: lead with the keyword/question; write the way a mum would search ("leaking after birth", "back pain physio singapore").
- **Hashtags:** 2-3 relevant, not 30. Mix niche (e.g. postnatalrecovery) + local (e.g. singaporephysio).
- **Stories:** behind-the-scenes, polls/Q&A (great for "is this normal?" prompts), reshare your own feed posts.
- **Engagement:** reply to every comment and DM fast; route booking intent to WhatsApp. DMs are a top conversion path - treat them as leads.
- **Don't:** before/after, superlatives, cure claims (Lane A); reposting patients' own posts. Fitness offers (Lane B) are fine in their own posts.

## Google Business Profile (highest-leverage local discovery)

**Role:** show up in "physio near me" and Maps, convert with photos + reviews + booking link.

- **Setup:** precise primary category (Physical therapy clinic) + secondaries (women's health clinic, sports medicine, fitness centre); complete hours; services; booking link; 10+ real interior/team photos; seed Q&A.
- **Posts:** 2-3x/week, repurposed from IG (tips, services, hours, workshops). Each with a CTA button (Book/Call/Learn more).
- **Reviews:** ask satisfied patients to share honest feedback - never incentivised, never scripted. Respond to every review professionally (replying lifts both ranking and conversion). Aim for steady velocity, not bursts.
- **Photos:** add fresh ones regularly (profiles with photos get materially more direction requests and clicks).
- **NAP:** Name/Address/Phone identical to the website and every directory.

## Local SEO + website (capture intent, convert)

**Role:** rank for condition + neighbourhood searches; convert visitors to bookings.

- **Condition landing pages** (build order): post-partum recovery, pelvic floor, back pain, neck/shoulder, then pre-natal, active-aging, Clinical Pilates, S&C. Each: reassurance hero + symptoms + how we help (educational) + what to expect + team + FAQ + location/hours + booking.
- **On-page:** keyword-led titles/H1s, plain-language body, internal links between related conditions, schema markup (LocalBusiness), fast mobile load.
- **Consistency:** NAP matches GBP exactly. Submit to relevant SG directories (and HealthHub/HCI directory if eligibility confirmed).
- **Patience:** 2-4 months to first movement; start day 1.

## Email (nurture + education + reactivation)

**Role:** stay top-of-mind, educate, bring lapsed patients back.

- **List hygiene:** consent-based; segment by condition/treatment/appointment type.
- **Cadence:** 1-2x/month. Education-led (Lane A); offers only on the fitness side (Lane B).
- **Plays:** new-patient welcome/education; monthly education note; workshop invite + recap; reactivation ("here's what's new"); post-visit follow-up.
- **Structure:** one hero education block + soft CTA. Personalise. Automate reminders where possible.

## WhatsApp (the SG booking + nurture workhorse)

**Role:** primary conversion and reminder channel (~98% open rate).

- **Consent first.** Then: greeting/auto-reply, FAQ quick-replies, booking flow, appointment reminders, gentle reactivation, post-visit check-in.
- **Speed wins:** fast replies convert enquiries to bookings. Tag the enquiry source.
- **Don't over-message.** Keep it warm, useful, human. Clinical replies stay factual (no cure claims).

## Partnerships & community (the post-partum engine)

**Role:** borrow trusted audiences in the women's-health ecosystem. See `../partnerships/partnerships-and-events.md`.

- Cross-referral agreements, co-hosted workshops, listing on partner resource pages, reciprocal social tagging.
- Seed genuinely useful content into mum communities via partners (not direct ads into invite-only groups).
- Pursue editorial round-up listings (Sassy Mama, HoneyKids, Little Steps) - editorial, not paid "best" endorsements.

## Paid media (modest, compliance-safe amplifier)

**Role:** capture high-intent search + amplify proven organic. Layered from month 2.

- **Google Search:** high-intent terms (women's physio, postnatal physio, pelvic floor physiotherapy, physiotherapy [neighbourhood], branded). Ad copy carries no prohibited claims (no best/guarantee/discount on Lane A).
- **Meta retargeting:** site + IG engagers. Lane A creative = education; Lane B = clean fitness offer.
- **Meta prospecting (light):** reach mums via neighbourhood, age, parenting/lifestyle interests, and lookalikes of engagers - not health-status targeting.
- **Budget discipline:** review cost-per-enquiry and cost-per-first-visit weekly; reallocate to the cheapest first-visit source.
- **Boosting:** put spend behind organic posts that already earned saves/shares (Lane-appropriate).

## The daily/weekly checklist

- **Daily:** reply to all DMs, comments, reviews, WhatsApp enquiries (tag sources).
- **Mon:** review leading indicators; set the week's posts.
- **Tue-Thu:** publish Reels/carousels + GBP posts.
- **Fri:** advance one partnership; check paid pacing; capture clinic moments.
- **Monthly:** batch film; BI north-star review; plan next month.
