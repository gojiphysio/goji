# Content Engine - Pillars, Cadence, Calendar, Idea Bank

The repeatable system that lets a small in-house team produce consistent, on-brand, compliant content. Pairs with `channel-playbooks.md`. Every piece passes the lane-aware checklist in `../strategy/03-compliance-guardrails.md`.

## Content pillars (and their target mix)

Mapped to the four strategic pillars. Suggested weekly/monthly mix:

| Pillar | Share | Lane | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Women's pelvic & perinatal health** (the wedge) | ~35% | A | Owns the category; drives partnerships and distinctiveness. |
| **MSK physio** (volume engine) | ~30% | A | Broadest reach + bookings; back/neck/shoulder/knee, desk & sport. |
| **Movement & fitness** (Pilates/S&C adjunct) | ~15% | B | Offers, return-to-movement, retention; freer creative. |
| **Active-aging & family** (quiet pillar) | ~10% | mixed | Strength with age, family breadth. |
| **Brand / behind-the-scenes / team** | ~10% | A | Warmth, trust, the practitioner faces. |

Across formats: ~40% practitioner education/myth-busting, 25% behind-the-scenes/warmth, 20% patient stories & "what to expect" (consented), 15% movement demos.

## Cadence (sustainable for a small team)

- **Start:** 2-3 Reels + 1-2 carousels + 1-2 static/week. Scale to 3 Reels + 2 carousels once the rhythm holds.
- **GBP:** 2-3 posts/week (repurpose Reels/carousels).
- **Email:** 1-2x/month (education + nurture).
- **WhatsApp:** reactive (fast replies) + occasional consented nurture.
- **Batch film:** one day/month produces 6-8 Reels. This is the single biggest lever for keeping cadence without burnout.
- **Best windows:** Wed/Thu, mornings and evenings SGT. Always on-screen text; 2-3 relevant hashtags; searchable keyword-rich captions.

## The repeatable workflow

1. **Pick** the week's pieces from the calendar (theme + pillar + format).
2. **Script** each Reel as 3 lines: hook / middle / takeaway.
3. **Batch film** monthly; edit in a simple tool; add captions + on-screen key points.
4. **Schedule** to the calendar; repurpose each piece to GBP and (where it fits) email.
5. **Engage** - reply to every comment/DM/review fast; route warm interest to WhatsApp booking.
6. **Review** weekly leading indicators; feed winners into the next batch.

## Repurposing map (one idea, many surfaces)

A single education topic becomes: 1 Reel (hook) -> 1 carousel (depth, saves) -> 2-3 GBP posts -> 1 email block -> WhatsApp FAQ snippet -> a section on the relevant landing page. Build once, distribute six ways.
