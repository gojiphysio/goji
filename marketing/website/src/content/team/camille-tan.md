Camille is a STOTT PILATES® certified mat and reformer instructor who is passionate about
helping people feel stronger and move better.

She sees Pilates as a way to build strength, improve mobility and deepen the connection
between mind and body through mindful, intentional movement.

Her teaching approach focuses on proper alignment, core stability, controlled breathing and
body awareness. Through clear guidance, exercise progressions and hands-on adjustments where
appropriate, Camille helps clients understand their movement and build confidence in their
abilities.

Camille welcomes people who are new to Pilates as well as those looking to deepen their
practice, and sessions move at each person's own pace.

For Camille, the most rewarding part of teaching is seeing clients grow stronger and more
confident, carrying what they learn in the studio into their everyday lives.
