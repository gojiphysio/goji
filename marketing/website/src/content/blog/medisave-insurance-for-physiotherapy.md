If you're seeing a private physiotherapist, MediSave usually won't pay for it. MediSave can go towards physio in a handful of set places: during a hospital stay, at an approved community hospital or day rehabilitation centre, and, if you're 60 or older, at a polyclinic, public specialist clinic or CHAS GP clinic when a doctor orders it. For private physio, insurance is the usual route. An Integrated Shield Plan may cover some rehab after a hospital admission, and company or personal outpatient plans vary a lot, so read your own policy before you book.

At Goji we don't direct-bill. You pay at the session and claim from your insurer afterwards.

## Can I use MediSave for physiotherapy?

Only in certain settings. CPF lists the kinds of rehabilitation MediSave can be used for, and each comes with its own cap ([CPF](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/healthcare-financing/using-your-medisave-savings)). The same page says MediSave can help with hospitalisation and day surgery bills, and physio you have on the ward is part of that bill.

| Where the physio happens | Can MediSave be used? | Cap set by CPF |
|---|---|---|
| On the ward, during a hospital stay or day surgery | Yes, as part of the hospital bill | Ask the hospital's billing team |
| Inpatient at an approved community hospital | Yes | $250 a day |
| Inpatient at an approved convalescent hospital | Yes | $50 a day, up to $3,000 a year |
| Outpatient at an approved day hospital | Yes | $150 a day, up to $3,000 a year |
| Outpatient at a day rehabilitation centre | Yes | $25 a day per rehabilitation service, up to $1,500 a year |
| Polyclinic, public specialist outpatient clinic or CHAS GP clinic, when a doctor orders it | Yes, through Flexi-MediSave, if you're 60 or older | $400 a year |
| A private physiotherapy clinic | Not under any of the schemes above | Not applicable |

The catch is in the word approved. MediSave can be used at public institutions and at private ones that are accredited for MediSave, and CPF publishes the list on the same page. Goji is a private physiotherapy clinic, which isn't one of the settings in that table, so MediSave can't be used for sessions with us.

## Can I use Flexi-MediSave for physio if I'm over 60?

Yes, if it's done in the right place. Flexi-MediSave is for CPF members aged 60 and above, and it covers outpatient treatment at polyclinics, public hospital specialist outpatient clinics and GP clinics on the Community Health Assist Scheme (CHAS). CPF says it can pay for physiotherapy "if ordered by your doctor" for a medical condition ([CPF](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/service/article/what-can-i-use-flexi-medisave-for)).

The yearly limit went up from $300 to $400 on 1 October 2025, and you can also use your spouse's MediSave if they're 60 or older too ([CPF](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/infohub/educational-resources/cpf-changes-announced-in-budget-and-cos-2025-and-what-it-means-for-you)). So if your mum's polyclinic doctor sends her for knee physio at the polyclinic, part of the bill may come out of MediSave. The same sessions at a private clinic wouldn't qualify.

## Does my Integrated Shield Plan cover physio after a hospital stay?

It can. An Integrated Shield Plan is MediShield Life with an extra layer of private insurance on top, aimed at higher ward classes or private hospitals ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/integrated-shield-plans/about-integrated-shield-plans/)). MOH describes these plans as covering large hospital bills, selected costly outpatient treatments, and "pre- and post-hospitalisation treatments" such as consultations, medication, tests and "some rehabilitative services" ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/plan-to-work-with-insurers-to-develop-integrated-shield-plans-covering-home-nursing-care-and-outpatient-rehabilitative-services/)). Physio after you're discharged can fall under that last part.

Apart from the Standard plans, coverage differs from one insurer to the next ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/managing-expenses/schemes-and-subsidies/integrated-shield-plans/comparision-of-integrated-shield-plans/)). Post-hospitalisation claims also don't go through MediSave. You send them to your insurer yourself, for reimbursement ([MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/financial-counselling-and-transparent-billing-process-in-place-to-assist-patients/)).

None of the official pages give a standard number of days or a standard list of conditions, because those sit in each policy. Before you book, ask your insurer:

- How many days after discharge post-hospitalisation treatment is covered
- Does the physio have to be for the condition you were admitted for
- Do they want a referral letter or memo from your doctor or specialist
- Does a private clinic count, and does a home visit

## What about company or personal outpatient insurance?

If your employer gives you medical benefits, or you've bought an outpatient plan yourself, that's the first place to look for private physio. Every plan is different. Some include physiotherapy and some don't, so look for it by name, and check whether there's a yearly cap or a limit per visit. Some insurers also need a doctor's referral letter before they'll reimburse physio, even though you don't need one to book.

Company cover has one more catch. MoneySense, the national financial education programme, points out that a group policy may end when you change employer or retire ([MoneySense](https://www.moneysense.gov.sg/health-insurance/)). If you're partway through a course of physio and about to switch jobs, check what happens to your claims first.

If the policy wording isn't clear, ask your HR team or the insurer directly, and get the answer in writing if you're planning a few sessions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does MediShield Life pay for outpatient physio?

MediShield Life is built for large hospital bills and selected costly outpatient treatments, such as dialysis and chemotherapy for cancer ([LIA](https://www.lia.org.sg/consumers/getting-started/healthcare/)). Outpatient physio at a private clinic isn't one of the treatments named. If your physio follows a hospital admission, ask whether your Integrated Shield Plan's post-hospitalisation benefit applies.

### Will Goji send the bill to my insurer?

No. We don't direct-bill, so you pay at the session and submit the claim to your insurer yourself.

### Can I claim for a home visit?

That depends on your policy. A home visit costs more than the same session at the clinic ($380 rather than $260), so ask your insurer whether they treat it the same way and whether there's a per-visit cap before you book.

### Can I use Flexi-MediSave at a private physio clinic?

No. CPF limits Flexi-MediSave to polyclinics, public hospital specialist outpatient clinics and CHAS GP clinics, so a doctor at one of those would need to order the physio there for it to count.
