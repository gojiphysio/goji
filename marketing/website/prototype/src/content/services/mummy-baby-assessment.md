A combined physiotherapy assessment for you and your baby, designed to support postpartum recovery and your baby's early development.

Suitable for babies from 0-12 months, and for mothers at any stage of postpartum recovery.

## Who is this for?

This assessment may be helpful if you:

- Want guidance on your recovery after pregnancy and birth
- Have concerns about your core, pelvic floor, scars, pain or return to exercise
- Want reassurance about your baby's movement and development
- Have questions about tummy time, head control, rolling or movement symmetry
- Have noticed a head-turning preference, flat spot or uneven movement
- Want practical advice for handling, carrying and playing with your baby

You do not need to have specific symptoms or concerns to book an assessment.

## What we assess

### For Mummy

Your assessment may include:

- Abdominal separation and core function
- Breathing and pressure management
- Pelvic floor strength, relaxation and coordination
- Bladder, bowel and prolapse symptoms
- Caesarean, perineal or episiotomy scar healing
- Pelvic, back, hip, neck, shoulder or wrist pain
- Posture, movement and functional strength
- Readiness to return to exercise or sport

### For Baby

Your baby's development check may include:

- Gross motor developmental milestones
- Head and neck control
- Tummy time, rolling and early movement skills
- Movement symmetry and overall movement quality
- Muscle tone and joint movement
- Screening for conditions such as torticollis, clubfoot and developmental dysplasia of the hip

## What happens during the appointment?

Your physiotherapist will discuss your medical history, pregnancy, birth, recovery, daily routines and any concerns about your baby's development.

Your assessment may include:

- Posture, breathing, abdominal and movement assessment
- Scar or pelvic floor assessment, where appropriate
- Observation of your baby's posture and spontaneous movement
- Age-appropriate developmental milestone checks
- Gentle assessment of muscle tone, joint movement and symmetry
- Practical observation of tummy time, carrying and positioning

An internal vaginal examination is always optional and will only be performed with your consent.

## What you will leave with

You will receive:

- A clear explanation of your assessment findings
- A personalised plan for your postpartum recovery
- Advice on core coordination, daily movement and return to exercise
- Positioning and handling guidance for your baby
- Tummy time and play recommendations
- Age-appropriate activities to support your baby's development
- Recommendations for follow-up care, where needed

## Your next steps

Depending on your assessment, we may recommend:

- Individual physiotherapy for Mummy or Baby
- Signature Postpartum Pathway
- [Clinical Pilates Reformer](/book?service=clinical-pilates)
- [Strength and Conditioning](/book?service=strength-conditioning)
- Referral to another healthcare professional, where appropriate
