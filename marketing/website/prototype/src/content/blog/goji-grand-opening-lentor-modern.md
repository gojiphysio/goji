Goji Women's and Family Physiotherapy is open at Lentor Modern, #01-13, next to Burger King and a 1-minute walk from Exit 1 of Lentor MRT on the Thomson-East Coast Line. We marked the opening with a grand opening event on Saturday 6 June 2026, and 50 people came along.

## What happened at the grand opening?

"Goji Physio: Grand Opening" was a celebration for the people who helped us get here. We invited professional partners, peers, family, friends and members of the community, including entrepreneurs, doctors, physiotherapists and industry partners, to meet the team and see the clinic for themselves. Families were welcome, and there were light refreshments.

Thank you to everyone who came, and to everyone who could not make it but sent good wishes.

## What does Goji offer?

Goji is a women's and family physiotherapy clinic. Our AHPC-registered physiotherapists provide:

- Women's health physiotherapy, including [pelvic floor](/services/pelvic-floor-physiotherapy/), [prenatal](/services/prenatal-physiotherapy/) and [postpartum](/services/postpartum-physiotherapy/) physiotherapy
- A [Mummy & Baby assessment](/services/mummy-baby-assessment/) that looks at your recovery and your baby's development in one appointment
- [Paediatric physiotherapy](/services/paediatric-physiotherapy/), from infancy to 18
- [Musculoskeletal and sports physiotherapy](/services/musculoskeletal-physiotherapy/)
- Schroth therapy for scoliosis, for [children](/services/schroth-scoliosis-children/) and [adults](/services/schroth-scoliosis-adults/)
- Vestibular rehabilitation and lymphoedema physiotherapy
- [Strength and conditioning](/services/strength-conditioning/) and [Clinical Pilates](/services/clinical-pilates/), led by our physiotherapists
- [Private Pilates](/services/private-pilates/), led by a STOTT PILATES certified instructor

Home visits are also available across Singapore. Durations and prices for each service are on its service page.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm. Sundays and public holidays are by appointment only.

### How do I get there from Lentor MRT?

Leave Lentor MRT by Exit 1, turn right, then left. We are next to Burger King, beside the Lentor Drive pick-up point, about a 1-minute walk. Our [getting here](/getting-here/) page has a short video for each route.

### Is there parking?

Yes, in the basement car park at Lentor Modern. The entrance is on Lentor Central. From the car park, take the rightmost lift to Level M (not Level 1), turn right and continue to the end of the row.

### Is the clinic accessible with a stroller or wheelchair?

Yes. We are on the ground floor with step-free access for wheelchairs and strollers via Lentor Drive. If you are coming by taxi or Grab, set Lentor Drive as your drop-off point: the clinic is beside it, with a sheltered walkway to our door.

### Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. Private physiotherapy in Singapore can be booked directly, and no referral is needed for our women's health appointments. Some insurers require a doctor's referral letter to reimburse physiotherapy, so check your policy if you plan to claim.
