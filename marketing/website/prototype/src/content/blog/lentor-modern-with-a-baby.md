Lentor Modern is directly connected to Lentor MRT station on the Thomson-East Coast Line, with basement parking and a sheltered drop-off on Lentor Drive. We could not confirm a nursing or baby-care room from an official mall source, so ask a staff member at the mall when you arrive rather than relying on a listing. For green space, Thomson Nature Park lists Lentor MRT as its transport option, and Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park, with its playgrounds, is a short walk from Bright Hill MRT, two stops south on the same line.

Everything below was checked against the developer's announcement, NParks and NLB in October 2026. Opening hours and closures change, so check the linked page before you set out.

## What are the essentials at Lentor Modern?

- MRT: the mall has direct connectivity to Lentor MRT station (TE5) on the Thomson-East Coast Line, according to [GuocoLand's opening announcement](https://www.guocoland.com.sg/Documents/PressReleases/2026/Media_Release_GuocoLands_Lentor_Modern_mall_opens_enhancing_the_Lentor_Hills_estate.pdf).
- Address: Lentor Central, Singapore 788887.
- Parking: basement parking, with the car park entrance on Lentor Central. Searching for Lentor Modern on Google Maps takes you to the correct entrance.
- Drop-off: the Lentor Drive pick-up point beside Lentor MRT Exit 1 is linked to the ground-floor shops by a fully sheltered walkway, and there is a second main drop-off point for the mall.
- Nursing or baby-care room: not confirmed by an official source at the time of writing. Ask a staff member at the mall for the nearest one.
- Baby supplies: the mall directory published at opening lists a Guardian pharmacy and a Cold Storage Fresh supermarket on Level 1.

## Which parks are near Lentor with a baby or toddler?

| Place | Getting there | Hours (NParks) | Access notes (NParks) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/bishan-ang-mo-kio-park) | 5-minute walk from Bright Hill MRT (TE7) | Open 24 hours | Listed as wheelchair accessible |
| [Lower Peirce Reservoir Park](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/park-detail/lower-peirce-reservoir-park) | Along Old Upper Thomson Road; nearest MRT is Bright Hill | Open 24 hours; boardwalk 7am to 7pm | Boardwalk is not wheelchair accessible |
| [Springleaf Nature Park](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/springleaf-nature-park) | 550m walk from Springleaf MRT (TE4), one stop north of Lentor | Open 24 hours | Listed as wheelchair accessible |
| [Thomson Nature Park](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/thomson-nature-park) | Between Old Upper Thomson Road and Upper Thomson Road; NParks lists Lentor MRT | 7am to 7pm | Wheelchair access only at the entrance node |

A few details from each NParks page:

- Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park has three [playgrounds](https://www.nparks.gov.sg/visit/parks/bishan-ang-mo-kio-park/amenities/playgrounds). The inclusive and adventure playgrounds are open 24 hours, and the inclusive playground is near disability-friendly washrooms with barrier-free access to Carpark A and bus stops. The water playground runs Thursday to Sunday and on public holidays, 8am to 11am and 4pm to 8pm. Some bridges in the park are closed for improvement works until December 2026.
- Lower Peirce Reservoir Park has a 900m wooden boardwalk through the forest. The Casuarina Entrance and the Cyathea boardwalk trail are closed for maintenance from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027; use the Jacaranda or Lower Peirce entrances for the rest of the boardwalk.
- Springleaf Nature Park is on Nee Soon Road, off Upper Thomson Road. From Springleaf MRT, NParks says to walk 550m against the traffic along Upper Thomson Road, past the Shell petrol station.
- Thomson Nature Park has a car park along Upper Thomson Road. Because wheelchair access is limited to the entrance node, a baby carrier may be easier than a pram if you plan to walk the trails.

Bring water, sun and insect protection, and plan around Singapore's afternoon storms. A park visit early in the morning or late in the afternoon is usually cooler for small children.

## Is there a library with a children's section nearby?

[Bishan Public Library](https://www.nlb.gov.sg/main/visit-us/our-libraries-and-locations/libraries/bishan-public-library) is the National Library Board branch we could confirm on NLB's site. NLB lists it as open Monday to Sunday, 10am to 9pm, closed on public holidays, with storytelling among its programmes. Check the [Children and Teens](https://childrenandteens.nlb.gov.sg/) site for upcoming sessions.

## What can we do on a rainy day?

- Stay indoors at Lentor Modern: the mall connects directly to Lentor MRT, and its opening directory lists cafes, a supermarket and children's enrichment centres.
- Take the Thomson-East Coast Line to Bishan Public Library for books and storytelling.
- Save the parks for a dry spell. Trails and boardwalks can be wet and slippery after rain, and the Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park water playground is outdoors.

## How do I get to Goji with a pram?

Goji Physiotherapy is on the ground floor of Lentor Modern at #01-13, next to Burger King and beside the Lentor Drive pick-up point. There is step-free access for wheelchairs and strollers via Lentor Drive.

- By MRT: leave Lentor MRT by Exit 1, turn right, then left. We are next to Burger King, beside the pick-up point, about a 1-minute walk.
- By taxi or Grab: set the Lentor Drive drop-off point as your destination. The clinic is beside it, with a fully sheltered walkway to our door.
- By car: park in the basement and take the rightmost lift. Select Level M (not Level 1, even though our unit is #01-13), turn right after the lift and continue to the end of the row.

Our [getting here page](/getting-here/) has a site plan and short video guides for each route. The clinic is on the ground floor and stroller-friendly, whether you are coming for a [Mummy and Baby assessment](/services/mummy-baby-assessment/) or [paediatric physiotherapy](/services/paediatric-physiotherapy/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Lentor Modern have a nursing room?

We could not confirm a nursing or baby-care room from an official Lentor Modern source. Ask a staff member at the mall for the nearest one when you arrive.

### Where do I park at Lentor Modern?

Lentor Modern has basement parking. The car park entrance is on Lentor Central, and searching for Lentor Modern on Google Maps takes you to it.

### What is the nearest MRT station to Lentor Modern?

Lentor MRT station (TE5) on the Thomson-East Coast Line, which connects directly to the mall. Exit 1 on Lentor Drive is the closest exit to the ground-floor shops by the pick-up point.

### Where is the nearest playground for toddlers?

Bishan-Ang Mo Kio Park has inclusive and adventure playgrounds open 24 hours, and a water playground from Thursday to Sunday and on public holidays. It is a 5-minute walk from Bright Hill MRT, two stops from Lentor.
