Kristy's clinical interests span pelvic health, postnatal rehabilitation, sports
physiotherapy, and paediatric care — including neurological cases. She enjoys working
across the full breadth of women's health and finds particular meaning in supporting
patients through the physical and emotional transitions of pregnancy and early parenthood.

She has a genuine interest in paediatric physiotherapy and works with infants and children
across a range of developmental, musculoskeletal, and neurological presentations. Kristy
approaches each case with careful attention to the whole person, and enjoys working with
families where multiple members may benefit from physiotherapy support.
