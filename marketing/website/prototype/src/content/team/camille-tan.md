Camille is a STOTT PILATES® certified mat and reformer instructor who is passionate about
helping people feel stronger, move better and develop greater confidence in their bodies.

She believes Pilates is about more than exercise. It is an opportunity to build strength,
improve mobility and deepen the connection between mind and body through mindful, intentional
movement.

Her teaching approach focuses on proper alignment, core stability, controlled breathing and
body awareness. Through clear guidance, thoughtful exercise progressions and hands-on
adjustments where appropriate, Camille helps clients better understand their movement and
build confidence in their abilities.

Whether someone is new to Pilates or looking to deepen their practice, Camille creates a
welcoming and supportive environment where everyone can learn, challenge themselves and
progress at their own pace.

For Camille, the most rewarding part of teaching is seeing clients grow stronger and more
confident, carrying what they learn in the studio into their everyday lives.
