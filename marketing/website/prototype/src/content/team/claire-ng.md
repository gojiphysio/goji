Claire joined Goji Health to be part of a team that puts the patient first in every sense.
She has a strong background in pelvic health and women's physiotherapy, and has worked with
patients navigating pregnancy, the early postnatal period, pelvic organ prolapse, bladder
and bowel concerns, and the physical changes that come with hormonal transitions.

She is also trained in lymphoedema management and vestibular rehabilitation, and brings the
same careful, patient-centred approach to all areas of her practice.

Claire is known for making patients feel genuinely heard and leaving sessions feeling
clearer about what is happening in their bodies and what comes next.
