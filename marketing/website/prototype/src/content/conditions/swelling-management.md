Swelling (oedema) is a build-up of fluid in the body's tissues, most often noticed in the legs, ankles, feet, or arms. It can follow surgery or injury, arise from problems with the lymphatic or venous systems, or accompany other medical conditions. Because the right management depends on the cause, the first step is a careful assessment. Physiotherapy at Goji Physio in Lentor, Singapore can play an important role in reducing and controlling swelling, protecting the skin, and restoring movement and comfort - working alongside your wider medical care.

## What causes swelling?

Swelling has many possible causes, including:

- **Post-surgical or post-injury swelling:** a normal part of healing that sometimes lingers or needs support
- **Lymphatic causes:** such as lymphoedema, when lymph drainage is reduced
- **Venous causes:** such as chronic venous insufficiency, when leg veins struggle to return blood
- **Prolonged immobility** or reduced activity
- **Other medical conditions** affecting the heart, kidneys, or circulation

Some causes of swelling need medical investigation, so assessment focuses on identifying what is driving it and whether onward referral is needed.

## When should swelling be assessed?

It is worth seeking assessment when swelling is persistent, uncomfortable, or limiting your movement. Seek prompt medical attention if swelling is sudden, painful, hot, or red - particularly in one leg - or is accompanied by breathlessness or chest pain, as these can indicate conditions needing urgent care.

## What does a session at Goji involve?

Your physiotherapist will take a history of your swelling, general health, and any recent surgery or injury, then assess the affected area. This may include:

- **Swelling assessment:** measuring and comparing the affected area and noting how it changes
- **Skin and tissue check:** looking at skin condition and tissue texture
- **Movement and function:** how the swelling affects your range and activity
- **Screening:** identifying any features that require medical review

You will leave with an explanation of what is likely contributing to your swelling and a management plan suited to the cause.

## How is swelling managed?

Depending on the cause, physiotherapy management may combine:

- **Manual lymphatic drainage:** a gentle technique to encourage fluid movement, where appropriate
- **Compression:** bandaging or garments to reduce and control swelling
- **Exercise and movement:** to support circulation and lymphatic flow
- **Elevation and self-management advice:** practical strategies to use day to day

Where swelling stems from a condition that needs medical treatment, physiotherapy works alongside your doctor's care rather than replacing it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is all swelling treated the same way?

No. Effective management depends on the cause - lymphatic, venous, post-surgical, or other. This is why assessment comes first, so treatment is matched to what is actually driving your swelling.

### Can compression help my swelling?

Compression is often helpful for lymphatic and venous swelling, but the right type and level need to be matched to you. Your physiotherapist will advise whether it is appropriate and guide its use.

### Should I keep the area elevated and rest?

Elevation can help, but prolonged rest is not always best - movement supports circulation and lymphatic flow. Your physiotherapist will guide the right balance for your situation.

### When is swelling a medical emergency?

Sudden, painful, hot, or red swelling - especially in one leg - or swelling with breathlessness or chest pain needs urgent medical attention. If in doubt, seek medical care promptly.

### Can post-surgical swelling be helped?

Yes. Swelling after surgery is common and usually settles, but where it lingers or limits recovery, physiotherapy techniques and guidance can help manage it and support your rehabilitation.
