Feeling less steady as we age is common, but falls are not an inevitable part of getting older - and much of the risk can be reduced. Instability often develops gradually from a mix of reduced strength, changes in balance and sensation, vision, medications, and confidence. Physiotherapy assessment at Goji Physio in Lentor, Singapore identifies which factors are most relevant to you and builds a targeted programme to improve strength, balance, and steadiness - helping you stay active, independent, and confident on your feet.

## What increases the risk of falls?

Falls usually result from several factors adding up, including:

- **Reduced leg and trunk strength** and slower reactions
- **Balance and inner-ear (vestibular) changes** affecting steadiness
- **Reduced sensation or proprioception** in the feet and legs
- **Vision changes** and poor lighting or hazards at home
- **Medications** that can cause dizziness or drowsiness
- **Fear of falling**, which can lead to less activity and, in turn, further loss of strength and confidence

Because the causes stack up, addressing several factors together tends to be more effective than focusing on any single one.

## When is a falls assessment worthwhile?

Consider an assessment if you or a loved one:

- Has had a fall or near-fall
- Feels unsteady, or holds onto furniture and walls
- Has become more cautious or fearful of falling
- Has reduced strength, or finds walking, stairs, or getting up from a chair harder
- Wants to stay active and independent and reduce future risk

## What does a session at Goji involve?

Your physiotherapist will discuss your health, activity, any falls history, and your goals, then carry out an assessment that may include:

- **Strength and mobility testing:** particularly the legs and trunk
- **Balance and gait assessment:** static and moving balance, walking, turning, and getting up from a chair
- **Vestibular screening:** checking whether the inner ear is contributing
- **Practical review:** discussing footwear, home hazards, and confidence

You will leave with a clear picture of your personal risk factors and a tailored programme to build strength, balance, and confidence.

## How does physiotherapy reduce falls risk?

The most effective fall-prevention programmes combine progressive strength training with challenging, individualised balance exercises, alongside practical advice on home safety and activity. Building capacity and confidence in this way is well-established as a way to reduce falls risk and support independence. Your physiotherapist will grade the exercises to keep you safely challenged.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are falls just an unavoidable part of ageing?

No. While risk can increase with age, falls are often preventable. Strength and balance are trainable at any age, and targeted programmes can meaningfully reduce risk.

### Will the exercises be safe if I'm already unsteady?

Yes - programmes are graded to your current ability, with appropriate support and safety measures. Building strength and balance is one of the best ways to become steadier.

### I've become afraid of falling - can that be helped?

Very much so. Fear of falling is common and can reduce activity, which worsens strength and balance over time. A graded, confidence-building programme helps address both the physical and the confidence side.

### Can you advise on my home environment?

Yes. Alongside exercises, your physiotherapist can discuss practical measures such as footwear, lighting, and reducing trip hazards to make your environment safer.

### Is this only for older adults?

While age-related instability is common in later life, anyone experiencing unsteadiness or a higher falls risk - for example after illness or a period of inactivity - can benefit from assessment.
