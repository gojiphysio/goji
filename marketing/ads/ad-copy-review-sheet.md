## What you're approving

Google Search ads across four ad groups, one per landing page. Each ad is a **Responsive Search Ad**: we supply many headlines and descriptions and Google automatically mixes them into live ads. **So every headline and every description must be true and make sense on its own** — any combination can show. Character limits (headline ≤30, description ≤90) are already validated; the count is shown in **[brackets]** after each line.

## Compliance standard already applied

All copy follows the AHPC advertising floor (`strategy/03`): **no** superlatives (best / leading / No.1 / only), **no** cure or outcome/time guarantees, **no** comparisons; “can help” framing; plain prices allowed. This has been pre-checked — your sign-off is the confirmation.

> **Note on one keyword:** you’ll see **“best pelvic floor physio singapore”** in AG1’s target list. We *bid* on it because people type it, but **the ad we show never uses the word “best.”** Bidding on a search term is targeting, not a claim — this is permitted under `strategy/03`.

> ### ⚠️ One item flagged for Cheryl
> **AG1 headline “Leakage Is Common & Treatable”** — “Treatable” is accurate (pelvic-floor physiotherapy is first-line care for incontinence) but is stronger than our usual “can help” wording. Please confirm it’s acceptable, or suggest an alternative.
