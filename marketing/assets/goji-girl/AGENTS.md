# Goji Girl — creative continuity

- Episode creative lives in the numbered episode folders. Keep the timed script,
  storyboard and creative decisions together; tooling and generation workflow
  belong to the separate workflow workstream.
- Carry forward the established adult protagonist: black ponytail, maroon
  athleisure, white sneakers and small black handbag. Goji staff wear black;
  the back of the top is plain, without lettering or logos.
- Outdoor action defaults to the third-person player camera: follow the girl
  from behind on foot and the vehicle from behind while driving. Keep the
  horizon level through vehicle roll. Indoor scenes can use staged cutscenes.
  Frame the girl as the controlled character even when pursuers enter the shot.
- Use the current `goji-gta-video` skill and its series bible / San Andreas
  references. Match original PS2 painted textures, economical faces and simple
  shading across every human and environment, not just the lead character.
- Episode 2 uses **SOS Transit**, retaining the established red/purple italic
  logo treatment and livery. Apply it to every visible bus wordmark.
- Vehicle door, reader and pole share the chassis transform. Validate the
  rotated entrance in Blender before stylising; reject a styled roof hatch or
  rear-door substitution even when the image otherwise looks good.
- Use the actual Goji frontage and Lentor Drive canopy photo references for
  exterior landmarks. Preserve the approved interior and explicitly label
  fictional geography needed for the gag.
- The episode-one Mobility popup is reusable verbatim. Episode 2 moves its
  1080×1920 box from y=180 to y=360; retain an editable overlay and review its
  clearance instead of baking generated lettering into the art.
- The comedy uses exaggerated game physics and deadpan reactions. Preserve the
  series' no-blood/no-deaths rule; incidental characters remain visibly unharmed.
- Label concept boards as concepts. The script controls timing, spatial logic
  and sound; generated frames are not proof of actual location geography.
- Hand off through a rendered GitHub Markdown page with embedded storyboard
  images and direct image links, following the Tidepool remote-first rules.
