# Goji Girl — creative continuity

- Episode creative lives in the numbered episode folders. Keep the timed script,
  storyboard and creative decisions together; tooling and generation workflow
  belong to the separate workflow workstream.
- Carry forward the established adult protagonist: black ponytail, maroon
  athleisure, white sneakers and small black handbag. Goji staff wear black;
  the back of the top is plain, without lettering or logos.
- The comedy uses exaggerated game physics and deadpan reactions. Preserve the
  series' no-blood/no-deaths rule; incidental characters remain visibly unharmed.
- Label concept boards as concepts. The script controls timing, spatial logic
  and sound; generated frames are not proof of actual location geography.
- Hand off through a rendered GitHub Markdown page with embedded storyboard
  images and direct image links, following the Tidepool remote-first rules.
