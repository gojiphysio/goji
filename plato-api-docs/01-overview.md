Plato Developer Platform

Reach out to the broader healthcare market through Plato's App Marketplace.

You will need developer resources on your end to build on top of Plato Developer Platform.

There are multiple ways to connect to Plato:

## 1) Plato API

Plato's API allows you to perform 'RESTful' operations such as reading, modifying, adding or deleting data from your Plato account.

See: API Overview (/developers/1.0/api)

## 2) Apps on Plato

Plato allows you to deploy private and partner apps that enhance the functionality and provide extra features on top of Plato.

See: Apps Overview (/developers/1.0/apps)

## 3) Lab/Radiology Linkage

Are you running a medical laboratory or a diagnostic imaging centre? You can stream your reports and scans directly to your customers through Plato. No more fax or couriers sending paper reports!
